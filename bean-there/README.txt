BEAN THERE - MOBILE COFFEE SHOP SIMULATOR

Vanilla JavaScript PWA. Serve this folder from a static HTTPS host (GitHub Pages) and open it on a phone in landscape.
The layout fits one screen with no scrolling. Install it to the home screen for standalone mode.

CONTROLS
- Drag cups from the shelf to a prep mat or an espresso group head.
- Drag a portafilter to the grinder, tap Grind dose (twice for a double), then drag it to the tamping mat and tap Tamp puck.
- Drag the tamped portafilter into a group head, put one or two cups under it, tap Brew.
- Drag a pitcher to the milk fridge and fill it, then to the steam wand and steam it. Drag it onto a cup mat to pour.
- Drag a cup to the syrup rail to add pumps. Drag finished cups onto the customer who ordered them.
- Drag spent portafilters to the knock box.

Coins buy automation and speed upgrades. Progress saves on this device. Bump V in sw.js on every deploy.
