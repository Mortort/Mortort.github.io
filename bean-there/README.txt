BEAN THERE — MOBILE COFFEE SHOP SIMULATOR

This is a vanilla JavaScript PWA. Serve the contents of this folder from a static HTTPS host (for example GitHub Pages) and open it in a mobile browser. Install/add it to the home screen for standalone PWA behavior. The manifest requests landscape orientation; browser support for orientation locking varies, especially on iOS.

CONTROLS
- Drag cups from the storage shelf onto one of the four cup mats.
- Drag portafilters to the grinder, tap Grind dose, and tap again for a second dose. Each grind runs and stops automatically.
- Drag ground portafilters to the tamping mat and tamp them.
- Drag a tamped portafilter into either espresso group head. Drag one or two cups into its cup positions and start extraction. Extraction stops automatically.
- A double dose fills one cup with two shots, or splits one shot into each of two cups.
- Drag pitchers to the milk fridge and fill them, then move them to the steam wand to steam.
- Drag a filled/steamed pitcher onto a cup mat to pour milk into that cup.
- Drag a cup to the syrup rail and tap pumps to add ingredients. Tap the latte-art bonus while the cup is at the rail.
- Drag a completed cup onto the matching customer to serve it.
- Drag spent portafilters to the knock box on the tamping station.
- Drag tools/cups back to the storage shelf when finished.

PROGRESSION
- Customers arrive one at a time, up to five waiting.
- Patience varies randomly. Five customers leaving ends the current shift; start another immediately.
- Recipes unlock gradually based on lifetime customers served.
- Coins buy equipment automation and capacity/speed upgrades.
- Lifetime stats, unlocked recipes, coins, and upgrades save in local storage on this device. No account or network connection is required after the app is cached.
